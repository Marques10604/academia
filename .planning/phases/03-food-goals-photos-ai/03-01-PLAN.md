## Phase Goal

**As a** user who wants more than a logger, **I want to** track protein lightly, set goals, get a morning-alarm guide, take progress photos and ask an AI about my data, **so that** I stay consistent and understand my progress.

This plan delivers the light protein tracking (ALIM-01, ALIM-02, ALIM-03). Plan 03-02 adds goals, the carbohydrate nudge (ALIM-04) and the photo reminder; plan 03-03 adds the alarm guide, the AI report and release 0.5.0.
