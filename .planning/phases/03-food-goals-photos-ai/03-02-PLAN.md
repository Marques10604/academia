## Phase Goal

**As a** user who wants more than a logger, **I want to** track protein lightly, set goals, get a morning-alarm guide, take progress photos and ask an AI about my data, **so that** I stay consistent and understand my progress.

This plan delivers the goals and gentle cues: weekly workout goal and streak (META-01), the no-guilt gap notice (META-05), the body-weight goal with a healthy pace (META-02), the carbohydrate nudge (ALIM-04) and the 4-week progress-photo reminder (CORPO-03).

<objective>
Turn the fixed weekly goal into a setting with a forgiving weekly streak, tell the user kindly how many days passed since the last workout, add a body-weight goal with the healthy pace and his real pace, suggest one more carbohydrate portion when the average weight stalls for 2 weeks, and remind him every 4 weeks to take front/side/back photos into his gallery.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criteria 1 (carb part), 2 and 4. TRAINING-SCIENCE 5.5 (weekly adjustment rule: under +0,10 kg/week for 2 weeks → add a carbohydrate portion; lean-bulk pace 0,25–0,4% per week), 6.3 (photos every 4 weeks, same place/light/pose); PITFALLS 13, 17, 18 (weekly goals with a grace week, no red or guilt, realistic goals, decide on the 7-day average).
Output: src/domain/week.js extended, src/domain/goals.js + tests, addDays in dates.js, Config 'Meta de treinos' card, Today streak / gap / photo reminder, Corpo 'Meta de peso' and 'Fotos de progresso' cards and the carb nudge, nudge on #/proteina, E2E 14-goals, sw.js re-stamped (version stays 0.4.0).
</objective>

Claude's discretion in this plan:
- Grace rule (D-06): a single week below the goal is skipped (it neither adds nor resets); two weeks IN A ROW below the goal end the streak. The current week counts only once it meets the goal (while in progress it is neither a hit nor a miss). Weeks before the week of the first completed workout are never misses. This is the most forgiving reading of "1 semana de folga permitida sem zerar" (PITFALLS 17) and is written on the Config card so the rule is visible.
- Weekly goal choices 1–6 (chips); anything else read from a backup falls back to 3.
- Gap notice (D-07) appears from 4 days (the normal gap at 3 workouts per week is 2–3 days), lives inside the existing "Treino de hoje" card (no new card), and from 10 days (RULES.LAYOFF_DAYS) adds that the suggested loads already come lighter (CARGA-05 behavior that exists since Phase 1).
- Weight goal (D-08) is set and shown on Corpo (where the weight lives), stored in settings 'weightGoal' = {targetKg, startKg, setAt}; the weekly-workout goal is set in Config; the protein target stays on #/proteina (03-01). Pace math in integer grams so the week counts never suffer from float rounding.
- Carb nudge (D-04) needs 2+ weigh-ins in both the current 7-day window and the window ending 14 days ago, and is silent when the user set a goal below his current weight. Threshold 0,2 kg in 2 weeks = TRAINING-SCIENCE 5.5 "< +0,10 kg/week for 2 weeks". Shown on Corpo's 'Peso' card (seen every morning through the check-in) and on #/proteina.
- Photo reminder (D-10): settings 'lastPhotosAt' (local date). Corpo always has the card; Today shows the small reminder only when due AND at least one workout is complete, so the first-day screen is not crowded (the Corpo card asks for the first photos from day one).
- No new store and no schema change: every new value is a settings key (already exported; 08-backup seeds weeklyGoal, weightGoal and lastPhotosAt since 03-01).
