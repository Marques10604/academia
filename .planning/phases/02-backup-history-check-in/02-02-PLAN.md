## Phase Goal

**As a** lifter with real data on my phone, **I want to** back up and restore my data and see my history, charts and a quick morning body check-in, **so that** nothing is lost and I can see my progress.

This plan delivers "see my progress" for training: the workout list, per-exercise history and the load chart.

<objective>
Add a History area: Today → "Histórico" → list of saved workouts by date (tap → its summary, with records computed only against earlier workouts) and the list of exercises → per-exercise history (dates, loads with kg/placa labels, reps) with a simple load-over-time SVG chart. The exercise screen links to it. The chart module is the app's own (~120 lines), themeable with the existing CSS variables, and is reused by the body-weight chart in 02-03.

Purpose: EVOL-01, EVOL-02, EVOL-04 and ROADMAP Phase 2 success criterion 4. Strength charts are the beginner's main evidence of progress in the first weeks (PITFALLS 18).

</objective>
